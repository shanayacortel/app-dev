# My Favorite Series: Alice in Borderland

*Genre:* Sci-Fi, Thriller, Survival  
*Created by:* Haro Aso (based on the manga)  

## About the Show
Alice in Borderland is a Japanese sci-fi thriller series on Netflix.  
It follows a group of friends who find themselves trapped in an empty version of Tokyo,  
where they must play deadly games to survive and earn "visas" to stay alive.

### Main Characters
- Arisu (Ryōhei Arisu)  
- Usagi (Yuzuha Usagi)  
- Chishiya (Shuntarō Chishiya)  
- Kuina (Hikari Kuina)  

“In this world, you have to fight to survive — or you disappear.”
